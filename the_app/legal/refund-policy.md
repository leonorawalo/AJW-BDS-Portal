## Today: no payments

The **AJW BAGS Portal** does not take, hold or pay out money. Programme participants are not charged any fee, subscription or other cost through the portal, and the portal stores no card, bank account or mobile-money details. As nothing is paid through the portal, there is nothing to refund through it.

Payments or agreements made with AJW outside the portal (for example under a separate contract for the {{programmeName}}) follow the terms of that agreement. Questions: [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}).

## If the portal ever handles money

AJW will not start taking or paying out money through the portal until this policy has been rewritten for it and published. The rewritten policy will tell you, before you pay:

- what you are paying for, the full price including taxes, and any other charges;
- who receives the money, and the licensed payment provider that processes it (for example a mobile-money or card provider);
- when and how you can cancel, and when you can get your money back;
- how to ask for a refund, and how long it takes;
- what receipt or tax invoice you get.

The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy will be updated at the same time, and the portal will ask you to accept the new versions before any payment feature appears.
