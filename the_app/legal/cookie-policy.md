This policy lists everything the **AJW BAGS Portal** website stores in your browser, and why. The list is based on what the portal's code actually does. It covers the website only. The Android app keeps its sign-in in the phone's private app storage, which other apps cannot read.

## Summary

- The portal sets **no cookies**.
- It uses **no analytics, advertising or tracking** of any kind.
- It keeps a few items in your browser's local storage, all **strictly necessary** for signing in, or for remembering your choice on the cookie banner.

## What is stored

| Name | Type | Category | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-cgnmnqjyvlgyndhmicjl-auth-token` | Local storage | Strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in (your sign-in session from Supabase, our sign-in provider). | Until you sign out, or are signed out after 30 idle minutes or when the session expires |
| `flutter.sb-cgnmnqjyvlgyndhmicjl-auth-token-code-verifier` | Local storage | Strictly necessary | A one-time security code used only while you "Continue with Google". | Removed when Google sign-in finishes |
| `ajw-cookie-consent` | Local storage | Strictly necessary | Remembers your choice on the cookie banner, and this policy's version. | Until you clear your browser's site data |

## Categories

- **Strictly necessary**: needed for the portal to work (signing in, security). Always on.
- **Functional**: would remember optional preferences. None are used today.
- **Analytics**: would measure how the portal is used. None are used today.

If AJW ever adds a functional or analytics item, it will be listed here first, this policy's version will change, and the portal will ask for your choice again. Nothing non-essential loads until you accept it.

## Third parties

The website loads its graphics engine and fallback fonts from Google's gstatic.com. Google receives your device's internet (IP) address to deliver them, but gstatic.com sets no cookies. When you choose to connect Google, or use "Continue with Google", you go to Google's own pages, which have their own cookie policy.

## Your choices

- Change your choice at any time from **Cookie settings** in the footer of any public page, or on the sign-in screen.
- You can also clear the portal's site data in your browser settings. This signs you out.
