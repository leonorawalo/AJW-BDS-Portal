
# AJW BAGS Portal: a plain-language guide

As of 5 October 2026.

BAGS helps small Kenyan businesses become ones a bank will lend to, and the portal is where that work is planned, done and checked.

## What BAGS is

**BAGS** stands for Business Advancement and Growth Services, a programme run by AJW Africa. A small business that joins gets about **12 months of free, hands-on help** from three consultants, each covering one area:

- **Legal and HR**: registration, licences, contracts, tax registration, staff policies.
- **Accounting and Tax**: a bank account, bookkeeping, budgets, tax returns, financial reports.
- **Marketing**: the business plan, brand, customers, sales and public relations.

AJW calls these three the **trio**. The programme has two milestones: the business should be a **going concern** within 3 months, and **bankable** (ready for a bank's services) by month 6. The rest of the year builds on that.

What the consultants must do, and the targets they're measured against, are written in AJW's **Terms of Reference (ToR)**: one per discipline, dated September 2022.

**Why the portal exists.** Before it, progress lived in spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads. Nobody could see at a glance which businesses were on track, what each consultant had done, or what was overdue. The portal puts every business, task, visit, document and meeting in one place, with the right view for each person. It runs in a web browser at portal.ajwafrica.org and as an Android app.

## Words you'll hear

| Word | What it means here |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise | A small business in the programme. The app also calls it a client or a business. |
| Owner | The person who runs the enterprise. |
| Trio | The three consultants on an enterprise: Legal, Accounting, Marketing. |
| Specialization | A consultant's discipline. It decides which ToR tasks they get. |
| ToR (Terms of Reference) | AJW's written brief for the consultants: what to do for each business, and the targets. |
| Portfolio | All the enterprises one consultant works on. AJW expects at least 30. |
| Going concern | A business that is properly set up and actually running, so it can be expected to carry on. Due within 3 months. |
| Bankable | Ready to use a bank's services, such as an account or a loan. Due by month 6. |
| Turnover | All the money a business takes in from sales in a year, before costs. KSh 50,000 a month is KSh 600,000 a year. |
| Collateral | Something of value a bank can hold as security for a loan: land, a vehicle, equipment. |
| CRB | Credit Reference Bureau: the agency that records whether a person or business repays debts. Banks check it. |
| KRA PIN | A tax number from the Kenya Revenue Authority. Needed for the business and the owner. |

| AGPO | Access to Government Procurement Opportunities: a certificate that lets small businesses bid for government work. |
| M&E | AJW's Monitoring and Evaluation team, who track the programme's results. |

## Who uses the portal

There are three kinds of account, and each sees a different portal. Nobody can sign up on their own: an Administrator invites every person by email. "Continue with Google" only signs in to an account that already exists.

- **Administrator**: AJW staff who run the programme. They see every enterprise and every person.
- **Consultant**: one of the trio. They see only the enterprises they're assigned to, and only their own discipline's tasks.
- **Enterprise Owner**: the business owner. They see only their own business.

| What | Administrator | Consultant | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Register an enterprise | Yes | No | No |
| Assign consultants | Yes | No | No |
| Invite, suspend or change people | Yes | No | No |
| See tasks | All disciplines | Their own discipline | All, on their business |
| Change a task's status | No, only follows it | Yes | Yes |
| Add a custom task | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Comment and attach documents | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Log visits | No | Yes | No, but sees them |
| Write recommendations | Yes | Yes | No, reads them |
| Edit business facts (turnover, start date, loan purpose) | Yes | Yes | No, reads them |
| Schedule Google Meet sessions | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Workshops | Yes | Yes | No |
| Programme measures, Documents, Users, Audit log | Yes | Their own measures only | No |

These rules are enforced by the database itself, not just by hiding buttons. A person who tried to get around the screens would still be refused.

## A business's year in the programme

Each business follows the same path, and the portal's programme clock counts the months from the day it joined.

| | Joining | Going concern | Bankable | Keep growing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **When** | Month 0 | Months 1 to 3 | Months 4 to 6 | Months 7 to 12 |
| **Deadline** | | Going concern due, month 3 | Bankable due, month 6 | Programme ends, month 12 |
| **For example** | Registered by AJW, trio assigned, owner invited, onboarding workshop | Business registered, KRA PIN and licences, bank account opened, books set up | Tax returns filed, tax certificate, monthly reports, bank statements and CRB check | Visits continue, advice continues, monthly reports |

The going-concern and bankable deadlines are counted from enrolment. They turn the clock red on the business's card when missed. The items in each phase are examples; the full list is the ToR checklist in Tasks. The ToR checklists stop at month 6, so the months 7 to 12 column is a reading, still to confirm with AJW.

## Module by module

Every module answers one question someone in the programme has. They're grouped by where you find them in the side menu.

### Inside an enterprise (everyone, on the enterprises they can see)

- **Dashboard**: "How close is this business to a bank loan?" It shows:
    - the **programme clock** ("Month 4 of 12"), which turns red when a milestone is overdue;
    - two scores out of 100 and the **KCB checklist** (out of 10);
    - **red flags** worth discussing;
    - **What's driving these scores**: each line names the task that turns it green.

    The scores update themselves as tasks are completed. Three **Business facts** are typed in by hand because no task can produce them: annual turnover, when the business started, and the loan purpose.
- **Tasks**: "What still has to be done?" The ToR tasks for each consultant's discipline appear by themselves, grouped into the going-concern phase (first 3 months) and the bankable phase (by month 6). Anyone can add a custom task. Tapping a task opens its status, comments and attached documents.
- **Visits**: "Are the consultants actually showing up?" Consultants log each visit or call: what happened and the next steps. The ToR expects 2 visits a month per discipline, and visits logged more than 2 days late are flagged.
- **Recommendations**: "What advice has the owner been given?" Consultants write advice with a priority, and mark it actioned once the owner acts.
- **Documents**: "Where's the certificate?" Uploaded files with a category: registration certificate, KRA PIN, licence, contract and so on. Files attached to a task appear here too.
- **Google files**: "Where's the shared plan?" Docs, Sheets and Slides created from the portal in the creator's Google Drive, shared with the owner and the assigned consultants.
- **Sessions**: "When do we meet?" Meetings booked on Google Calendar with a Google Meet link. The portal checks people are free first, and cancelling removes the meeting from everyone's calendar.
- **Details** (Admin only): the business's registration details, its stage, the **going concern** switch, the owner's login account, and its consultants.

In the top bar inside an enterprise:

- **Email**: write to the people on this enterprise from your own Gmail, or search Gmail for past emails with them.
- **Reports**: the loan-readiness report as a PDF, or as a Google Doc, data Sheet or progress Slides deck.

### Administrator pages

- **Enterprises**: every business as a card, with its status and programme clock. Register a new one here, or export the whole portfolio to Google Sheets.
- **Programme**: AJW's scoreboard against the ToR targets, such as "90% of businesses registered". It also covers portfolio size against the minimum of 30, going concern within 3 months (target 50%), survival, and visits. It's for AJW's own oversight, and the figures to quote to partners or funders.
- **Workshops**: onboarding and induction workshops, who attended, and each registration list submitted to AJW.
- **Documents**: programme files that belong to no single business. Today that's the workshop registration lists.
- **Users**: invite people, set their role and discipline, suspend access.
- **Audit log**: a permanent record of key changes: who changed what, and when. Nobody can edit it.

### Consultant pages

- **My portfolio**: their enterprises as cards, plus their own ToR measures.
- **Monthly report**: the BDS Status Report, Activity Report and next month's Workplan the ToR asks for by the 3rd, as one Google Doc.
- **Workshops**: as for Administrators.

**A word on the scores.** Business Health and Credit Readiness are this portal's own guide, built from KCB's published small-business loan criteria. The KSh 500,000 turnover line is one of them. They are not in the ToR, and AJW should confirm them before anyone relies on them.

## Scenarios

These are the everyday situations, step by step, as the portal handles them today.

### A new business joins

1. The Administrator opens **Enterprises** and taps **Register enterprise**: business name and owner's name, plus optional county, industry, registration number and KRA PIN.
2. Inside the new enterprise, **Assign consultant** picks one Legal, one Accounting and one Marketing consultant.
3. In **Details**, the Administrator invites the owner by email (or links an existing owner account).
4. The owner gets an email, taps the link, and chooses a password (8+ characters, with upper and lower case, a number and a symbol).
5. The owner lands on their Dashboard. A short welcome tour shows them around, and the portal offers to connect their Google account (they can choose "Later").

### A consultant starts on a business

1. The consultant gets a phone notification if they use the Android app, and a red dot appears on the business's card.
2. They open **Tasks**. Their discipline's ToR checklist is already there, grouped into the first 3 months and month 6.
3. After a visit, they **Log a visit** in Visits within 2 days.
4. When a piece of work is done, for example the business is registered, they open the task and set it to **Completed**. On the Dashboard, "Business registered" turns green and the scores rise.
5. Advice for the owner goes under **Recommendations**.

### The owner checks in

1. The owner opens the portal and sees their scores and the programme clock.
2. They open a task and ask a question in the comments. Their consultant sees a red dot on Tasks.
3. They upload a document, such as a new licence. It appears under Documents for everyone on the business.

### Booking a meeting

1. Anyone connects their Google account once, from **Sessions**.
2. **Schedule** picks the people, a time and a title. The portal checks their calendars for clashes.
3. Google Calendar creates the meeting with a Meet link and emails the invitations. Invitees see a red dot on Sessions.
4. If the organiser cancels, the meeting disappears from everyone's calendar.

### Running a workshop

1. A consultant or Administrator creates the workshop under **Workshops**.
2. On the day, or afterwards from the sign-in sheet, they add each attendee.
3. Within 5 days they tap **Submit list**. An Excel file lands in the Administrators' **Documents**, with a red dot.
4. If it isn't submitted in time, the workshop turns red and Administrators see a red dot.

### A consultant is replaced

1. The Administrator assigns a new consultant for that discipline.
2. The old consultant's assignment ends by itself, and the business leaves their portfolio.
3. Every unfinished task of that discipline moves to the new consultant. Completed tasks stay credited to whoever did them.

### Someone forgets their password

1. On the sign-in page they tap **Forgot password** and enter their email.
2. They get an email, tap the link and choose a new password. The link works once and lasts 24 hours.

### Month end

1. Each consultant opens **My portfolio** and taps **Monthly report**. It defaults to last month.
2. One Google Doc is created with the Status Report, Activity Report and next month's Workplan, due by the 3rd.

## Red dots, notifications and tips

The portal tells people what needs them in three ways. None of them ever block work.

**Red dots** sit on a menu item or a business's card when something new arrived that **someone else** did. Opening that place clears the dot. There's no number and no pop-up.

| Who | A dot appears when | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consultant | They're assigned to a new business | My portfolio and that business's card |
| Consultant | Someone else adds a task for them, or comments on their task | That business → Tasks |
| Owner | A comment on a task, a new recommendation, or a document someone else uploaded | The matching section |
| Anyone | They're invited to a meeting someone else booked | That business → Sessions |
| Administrator | An invited person accepted | Users |
| Administrator | A workshop list was submitted, or went overdue | Documents, Workshops |

**Phone notifications** reach people who use the Android app, for example when a consultant is assigned or a task is completed. A completed task that moves the scores says "Loan readiness improved".

**Tips** are short guided tours. The first time a person reaches a page, the screen dims, a spotlight circles a button, and a bubble explains it in two or three sentences. Each tour shows once per account, can always be skipped, and can be turned off. **Show tips for this page** in the menu replays it any time.

## Edge cases

Most everyday edge cases are handled, but there are real gaps worth knowing before handover.

### Handled

| Situation | What the portal does |
| --- | --- |
| An invite fails on the first try (weak network) | Retries once by itself, without sending two emails |
| An Admin picks the wrong role or discipline | Asks "Invite Name as a Legal Consultant?" before sending |
| Changing a consultant's discipline while they have businesses | Refused, with what to do: end or reassign those first |
| Assigning a consultant who has no discipline | Refused, with how to fix it |
| Assigning a second consultant for the same discipline | Ends the old assignment; unfinished tasks move to the new person |
| An owner whose login isn't linked to a business | Says so, and to contact their AJW administrator |
| A suspended account | Can't sign in |
| Someone types another role's page address | Sent back to their own home page |
| A consultant opens a business they aren't assigned to | Sees nothing, enforced by the database |
| An invite or reset link older than 24 hours | The link stops working; the email itself says to ask for a new one |
| "Continue with Google" with an email that has no account | "No AJW account uses that Google email" |
| A Google connection expires or is revoked | Drops it and shows Connect again |
| A meeting would clash with someone's calendar | Warns before booking |
| The same ToR task added twice | Impossible: each ToR item exists once per business and discipline |
| A visit dated in the future | Refused |
| A workshop list goes past 5 days | Turns red, and Admins get a red dot |
| A name typed like a spreadsheet formula | Kept as text in the Excel list, so it can't run |
| One phone shared by two people | Notifications go only to whoever signed in last |
| Red dots or tips can't load | Quietly skipped; the rest of the page works |
| A long side menu on a short window | The menu scrolls above its footer and shows the selected section |

### Not handled yet

| Gap | What happens | Possible fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No signal during a field visit | Nothing can be saved offline; the visit is logged later | An offline mode (large job) |
| An owner with two businesses | They only see one | Let owners switch between their businesses |
| Going concern is a switch an Admin flips | Nothing checks it against the tasks | Suggest it when the going-concern tasks are done |
| Marketing tasks | Don't affect any score | Decide with AJW whether they should |
| The score model and the KSh 500,000 line | Not from the ToR; from KCB's public criteria | AJW confirms or changes the numbers |
| The Android app | Only changes when a new APK is built and installed; today it lacks the tips, the new workshop flow and the loan-purpose list | Build 1.2.1 |
| iPhone | No app; the website works in Safari | Fine for now |
| Database backups | The free Supabase plan keeps none | AJW decides on a paid plan or regular manual backups |
| Google review (submitted 5 Oct) | Until approved, people see an "unverified app" warning, and at most 100 people can connect Google | Wait for Google's answer, 4–6 weeks |

Automated tests (51) cover the scoring, the ToR checklists, the tips, the CSV, the menus and the side menu at short heights. The database rules were tested as real Admin, Consultant and Owner accounts. What's missing is a full click-through of every screen on a real Android phone.
