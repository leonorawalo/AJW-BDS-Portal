Privacy Policy

Version 2.1 · Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Effective: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how the AJW BAGS Portal ("the portal", "we", "us"), operated by Apprentice Job Work Africa (trading as AJW Africa) for its Business Advancement and Growth Services (BAGS) programme, collects, uses, stores and shares personal data. It includes how we handle information received from Google when you choose to connect your Google account.

Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 (the Act) applies to the portal. It is enforced by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

1. Who is responsible

Apprentice Job Work Africa is the data controller for the portal.

  • Address: Block D, Merchant Square, Riverside Drive, Nairobi
  • Data protection contact: ajw.bags.portal@gmail.com
  • Email: ajw.bags.portal@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Account information

Your name, email address, role (administrator, consultant or enterprise owner) and, for consultants, your specialisation (Legal, Accounting or Marketing). AJW programme administrators create accounts by invitation. You may add your phone number in Settings and privacy (optional); AJW administrators and the people you work with in the programme can then see it.

Programme information

Details of the enterprises in the programme:

  • business name, owner name and contact details (email, phone, county);
  • registration and tax details (registration number, Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) personal identification number (PIN));
  • financial facts such as annual turnover, business start date and loan purpose;
  • consultant assignments, tasks and comments, and visit notes;
  • documents you upload (for example licences, certificates, registration papers, bank statements);
  • recommendations, success stories and monthly reports;
  • meetings, and meeting notes and transcripts when that feature is switched on.

Messages

Chat messages and attachments you send in the portal, and emails you write in the portal (sent from your own Gmail account).

Workshops

For programme workshops, the names, phone numbers and business names of attendees, as entered by AJW staff.

Device and technical information

  • If you use the Android app and allow notifications, a push-notification token for your device. It is used only to deliver portal notifications to you.
  • On the website, the browser storage described in our Cookie Policy. The portal uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

Activity records

A record of key actions, for accountability and security. Examples: a consultant being assigned, a task being completed, a document being uploaded, opened or downloaded. Only programme administrators can see these records.

Consent records

What you agreed to (the Terms of Service, that you read this policy, consent to storage outside Kenya, that you are 18 or over, and optional programme emails), with the version and the time.

Sensitive personal data

Some programme records can include sensitive personal data as the Act defines it, mainly property details (for example assets, bank statements and title documents of a sole proprietor) and family details (for example a family member named in a success story). We use it only to deliver the programme, protect it as described in section 8, and store it outside Kenya only with your consent (section 6).

What you must provide

Your name, email address and role are needed to create your account. For enterprises, the business details and documents in the programme checklist are needed to deliver the programme; without them we cannot assess or support the business. Connecting Google and receiving programme emails are optional.

3. Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you connect one, the portal requests only the permissions below, and uses them only for the features you use:

Permission What the portal does with it
Basic profile and email (openid, email, profile) Shows which Google account you connected, and lets you sign in with Google to an existing portal account.
Google Calendar events (calendar.events) When you schedule a meeting in the portal, creates one event with a Google Meet link on your own calendar and invites the participants you chose. When you cancel it, deletes that event. The portal does not read, list or change any other events.
Calendar free/busy (calendar.freebusy) Checks whether you and the meeting participants are free at the time being booked. Only "busy" or "free" is read, never event details.
Google Drive files created by the portal (drive.file) Creates the Google Docs, Sheets and Slides you ask for (reports and working documents) in your own Drive, in an "AJW BAGS Portal" folder, and opens or exports them. The portal can only see files it created or that you opened with it, never the rest of your Drive.
Send email as you (gmail.send) Sends an email you wrote in the portal from your own Gmail account to the recipients you chose. The portal cannot read, search or delete any of your email.

Limited Use. The AJW BAGS Portal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Google user data:

  • is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features described above;
  • is never sold, and never used for advertising, including personalised or retargeted advertising;
  • is not used to build or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models;
  • is not read by people, except with your explicit consent for a specific purpose (for example, investigating a problem you reported), when needed for security, or when required by law;
  • is transferred to others only as needed to provide the feature you used (for example, the meeting invitations Google sends to participants you chose).

How Google credentials are stored

When you connect Google, the portal's server stores a Google refresh token so it can act on your behalf when you use a Google feature. It is kept only on the server (the app on your device never receives it), in a database table that ordinary portal users cannot access, encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. It is deleted when you disconnect Google in the portal, and becomes unusable if you remove the portal's access in your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

5. Who can see your information

  • Programme administrators can see all programme records.
  • Consultants can see the enterprises they are assigned to, and the tasks in their own specialisation.
  • Enterprise owners can see their own enterprise's records.
  • Meetings are visible only to their organiser, participants and administrators.
  • Chats are visible only to the people in that conversation.

The database itself enforces these rules, not only the app. We do not sell personal data.

8. How we protect it

  • Access is by invitation only.
  • Data travels over encrypted connections and is encrypted at rest by our providers.
  • The database checks each person's role on every request.
  • Documents are stored privately and opened through links that expire within 5 minutes. Every view, upload and download is logged.
  • Inactive sessions sign out after 30 minutes.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your personal data at risk, we will tell the ODPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell you without undue delay, as section 43 of the Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • access your personal data;
  • object to the processing of all or part of it;
  • have false or misleading data corrected;
  • have false, misleading or unlawfully processed data deleted;
  • receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (data portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing done before.

In the portal, go to Settings and privacy. There you can download your data, ask for a correction, ask for deletion of your data, and change your email preferences. You can also email us at ajw.bags.portal@gmail.com.

We answer requests to access your data within 7 days, and requests to correct or delete it within 14 days. If we refuse a request, we tell you why.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner: www.odpc.go.ke.

10. Children

The portal is for adults (18 or over) taking part in a business programme. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. A success story may mention someone under 18. If so, it is saved only after the consultant confirms that a parent or guardian has consented, as section 33 of the Act requires.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes in substance, we update its version and date, and the portal asks you to read and accept it again.
