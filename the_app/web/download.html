Get the BAGS Portal

Use it on your Android phone, or in any web browser.

On an Android phone

Download the Android app

Version 1.2.1, 6 October 2026. From this version on, the app tells you when a newer one is out.

For most phones from the last several years. If the app won't install on an older phone, try the version for older (32-bit) phones.

  1. Tap Download the Android app. Your phone may say the file could be harmful. That's normal for apps that don't come from the Play Store; choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open BAGS Portal and sign in with the email address your invitation was sent to.

Already set your password from the invitation email? Use the same password in the app.

Updating? Download and install it over the app you have; your sign-in stays. Only a test version from before 3 October 2026 has to be uninstalled first.

In a web browser (any phone or computer)

Go to portal.ajwafrica.org and sign in. Nothing to install, and it works on iPhone too.

Want an icon on your home screen? You can add the website like an app. It opens in its own window and always shows the latest version:

  • Android (Chrome): open the site, tap the menu (⋮), then Add to home screen or Install app.
  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): open the site, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or the menu, then Install.
