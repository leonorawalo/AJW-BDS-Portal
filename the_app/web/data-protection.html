This short notice explains how [AJW REGISTERED NAME] ("AJW", "we") handles personal data in the AJW Business Advancement and Growth Services (BAGS) Portal, under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (the Act). Our Privacy Policy gives the full detail.

Who is responsible

[AJW REGISTERED NAME] is the data controller.

  • Registration with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC): [ODPC REGISTRATION NUMBER]
  • Data protection officer: [DATA PROTECTION OFFICER CONTACT]
  • Email: ajw.bags.portal@gmail.com

What we collect

  • About you: your name, email address and your role in the programme.
  • About the enterprise:
    • business details, and registration documents, licences and certificates;
    • tax and bank evidence, and financial facts;
    • tasks, visit notes, meeting records and messages.
  • Technical: when you sign in, and which documents you open, upload or download (kept in an audit log).

Why, and on what basis

To run the BAGS programme: follow each enterprise's progress, coordinate its consultants, prepare it to apply for bank loans and report on the programme. We rely on the programme agreement with the enterprise, our legitimate interests in running and measuring the programme, and your consent where we ask for it.

Who can see it

Only the enterprise's owner, its assigned consultants and AJW administrators can see an enterprise's information, and the database itself enforces this. Documents are stored privately and opened through links that expire within minutes. We do not sell personal data.

Where it is stored

With our service providers, Supabase (European Union) and Google. The Privacy Policy lists them, and the safeguards we use for transfers outside Kenya.

How long we keep it

For the programme, and afterwards for as long as AJW's reporting obligations require. Administrators set retention periods in the portal. [AJW RETENTION SCHEDULE]

Your rights

You can:

  • get a copy of your data;
  • ask for corrections, or for your data to be deleted;
  • object to processing, or withdraw your consent.

Use Settings and privacy in the portal's menu. We answer requests to access your data within 7 days, and requests to correct or delete it within 14 days. You may also complain to the ODPC (www.odpc.go.ke).
