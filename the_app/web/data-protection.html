Data Protection Notice

Version 2.1 · Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Effective: 3 November 2026

This short notice explains how Apprentice Job Work Africa ("AJW", "we") handles personal data in the AJW Business Advancement and Growth Services (BAGS) Portal, under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (the Act). Our Privacy Policy gives the full detail.

Who is responsible

Apprentice Job Work Africa is the data controller.

  • Data protection contact: ajw.bags.portal@gmail.com
  • Email: ajw.bags.portal@gmail.com

What we collect

  • About you: your name, email address and your role in the programme.
  • About the enterprise:
    • business details, and registration documents, licences and certificates;
    • tax and bank evidence, and financial facts;
    • tasks, visit notes, meeting records and messages.
  • Technical: when you sign in, and which documents you open, upload or download (kept in an audit log).

Some records can be sensitive personal data under the Act, mainly property details and family details.

Why, and on what basis

To run the BAGS programme: follow each enterprise's progress, coordinate its consultants, prepare it to apply for bank loans and report on the programme. We rely on the programme agreement with the enterprise, the engagement contracts of consultants and staff, our legitimate interests in running and measuring the programme, and your consent where we ask for it (optional emails, and storing sensitive personal data outside Kenya).

Who can see it

Only the enterprise's owner, its assigned consultants and AJW administrators can see an enterprise's information, and the database itself enforces this. Documents are stored privately and opened through links that expire within minutes. We do not sell personal data.

Where it is stored

With our service providers, Supabase (European Union) and Google, so outside Kenya. We do this with contractual safeguards and, for sensitive personal data, your consent, as sections 48 and 49 of the Act require. The Privacy Policy gives the details.

How long we keep it

Programme records: 7 years after the enterprise leaves the programme. Uploaded documents: 3 years after it leaves. Chat messages: 2 years. Accounts: closed when you leave, and personal details removed 1 year later. The Privacy Policy has the full schedule.

Your rights

You can:

  • get a copy of your data;
  • ask for corrections, or for your data to be deleted;
  • object to processing, or withdraw your consent.

Use Settings and privacy in the portal's menu. We answer requests to access your data within 7 days, and requests to correct or delete it within 14 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) (www.odpc.go.ke).
