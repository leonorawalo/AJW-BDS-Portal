AJW BAGS Portal

Business Advancement and Growth Services · AJW Africa

The AJW BAGS Portal is the working tool for AJW Africa's Business Advancement and Growth Services (BAGS) programme. It supports micro, small and medium enterprises in Kenya working towards becoming a Going Concern and loan ready. It brings together the enterprise, its assigned business consultants (Legal, Accounting and Marketing) and AJW's programme administrators in one place.

What the portal does

  • Enterprise records for each business in the programme, and its progress through the programme.
  • Consultant assignment: each enterprise gets a Legal, Accounting and Marketing consultant.
  • Tasks and checklists based on the programme's terms of reference, with comments and document uploads.
  • Loan-readiness dashboard: Business Health and Credit Readiness scores, lender requirements met, and red flags, calculated from completed work. The scores are guidance, not a credit decision.
  • Meetings: consultation sessions scheduled on the organiser's own Google Calendar with a Google Meet link, after checking participants' availability.
  • Reports: loan-readiness reports as PDF, or as Google Docs, Sheets and Slides created in the user's own Google Drive.
  • Email: messages to the people working on an enterprise, sent from the user's own Gmail account.
  • Chat between the people working on an enterprise.
  • Notifications when consultants are assigned or tasks are completed.

Who can use it

Access is by invitation only. AJW Africa's programme administrators create accounts for enterprise owners, consultants and staff taking part in the BAGS programme. Connecting a Google account is optional. You only need it for the meeting, report and email features above.

Cost

The portal is free for programme participants and takes no payments. See our Refund Policy.
