AJW BAGS Portal

Business Advancement and Growth Services · AJW Africa

The AJW BAGS Portal is the working tool for AJW Africa's Business Advancement and Growth Services (BAGS) programme. It helps micro, small and medium enterprises in Kenya become a Going Concern and loan ready, by bringing together the enterprise, its assigned business consultants (Legal, Accounting and Marketing) and AJW's programme administrators in one place.

What the portal does

  • Enterprise records for each business in the programme, and its progress through the programme.
  • Consultant assignment: each enterprise gets a Legal, Accounting and Marketing consultant.
  • Tasks and checklists based on the programme's terms of reference, with comments and document uploads.
  • Loan-readiness dashboard: Business Health and Credit Readiness scores, lender requirements met, and red flags, calculated from completed work.
  • Meetings: consultation sessions scheduled on the organiser's own Google Calendar with a Google Meet link, after checking participants' availability.
  • Reports: loan-readiness reports as PDF, or as Google Docs, Sheets and Slides created in the user's own Google Drive.
  • Email: messages to the people working on an enterprise, sent from the user's own Gmail account.
  • Notifications when consultants are assigned or tasks are completed.

Who can use it

Access is by invitation only. Accounts are created by AJW Africa's programme administrators for enterprise owners, consultants and staff taking part in the BAGS programme. Connecting a Google account is optional and only needed for the meeting, report and email features above.
